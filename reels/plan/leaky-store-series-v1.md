# "The Leaky Store" — content series ideas (11 Nov 2026)

Core story (opinion): ads bring people to the store; the store has to do the selling. More ad spend into a store that doesn't answer shoppers' questions = pouring water faster into a leaking bucket.

Recurring device: the same founder character and the same store, drawn as a bucket/shop with holes. Each episode plugs one hole. Every caption ends: "Comment STORE and we'll find your leak."

## Episodes (one leak each)
1. Pilot — "More ads won't fix this." Fact: average documented cart abandonment is 70.22% (Baymard, 50 studies, page updated 28 Oct 2026).
2. No reviews. Fact: a product with five reviews is 270% more likely to be bought than one with none; +380% for higher-priced items, +190% for lower-priced (Spiegel Research Center, Northwestern; first published 2017).
3. Perfect 5.0 / fake-looking reviews. Fact: purchase likelihood typically peaks at 4.0–4.7 stars; marking verified buyers improves the odds of purchase by 15% (Spiegel). Fact: India's BIS standard IS 19000:2022 covers fake and undisclosed paid reviews; DoCA has said fake reviews can be treated as unfair trade practice (status of making it mandatory not confirmed after Nov 2023).
4. Bad product photos. Fact: 56% of test users first explored product images on arrival; 42% tried to judge size from images; 28% of 60 major sites had no in-scale images (Baymard, 2017).
5. Not guiding them. Opinion: answer "which one is for me?" — best-seller tag, a 3-row comparison, a 2-question quiz. Possible debunk tie-in: the famous jam study (24 vs 6 jams) — later meta-analysis found the choice-overload effect inconsistent (verify before scripting).
6. Not trusted at payment. Fact: 19% abandoned because they didn't trust the site with card details (Baymard, US). Fix: UPI/COD icons, return policy and WhatsApp contact next to the Pay button.
7. Ad promise ≠ landing page. Opinion: if the ad shows one product, land on that product, not the homepage.
8. The 10-second legitimacy check. Hypothesis (no data found): shoppers from an ad check the brand's Instagram before paying an unknown store. Test it: run a story poll/question to our own audience and use the real answer as content.

## Evergreen framework post (opinion)
The 5 questions every shopper asks before paying: What is it? Is it for me? What does it cost, all in? When will it arrive? What if I don't like it? — carousel, one question per slide, each with the page element that answers it.

## Episode 1 (pilot) — script v1 (11 Nov 2026)
[serious] Ten thousand rupees on ads. Three hundred visitors. … [curious] Zero orders. … So… more ads? [serious] Here's the problem. On average, seven out of ten online carts get abandoned. That's Baymard's average across fifty studies. [warmly] Ads fill the bucket. Your store has to hold the water. No reviews. Blurry photos. A surprise charge at checkout. Every one is a hole. [serious] You don't have a traffic problem. You have a leak. Comment STORE… and we'll find yours.
- ₹10,000 / 300 visitors / zero orders = illustrative example, labelled on screen.
- Fact: Baymard average documented cart abandonment 70.22% (50 studies, page updated 28 Oct 2026).
