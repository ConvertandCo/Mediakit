# 30-day Instagram series: "The Leaky Store" (16 Nov – 15 Dec 2026)

Brand: CONVERTNCO / Convert&Co · Niche: Shopify CRO for Indian D2C brands
Audience (ICP Profile A, hypothesis): founders/marketing leads of Indian Shopify D2C brands (fashion, beauty, home, food) already running Meta ads; 24–45. Pain in their words (not yet heard from prospects): "Ads get clicks but nobody buys."
Offer: free audit (~30 min of our time, capped) → CRO sprint ₹35–50k.
Goal: leads (audit requests), not followers. Tone: direct, calm, evidence-first, a little wry. Voiceover + SFX only, no music.

## Volume (opinion, read first)
2–3 brand-new reels a day is not realistic for two people making animated reels. Plan instead:
- Reel 1 daily = HERO (the series episode). About half already exist or are scripted.
- Reel 2 daily = TEST VARIANT of a hero (alt hook or alt style), posted as a Trial Reel to non-followers. Same body, ~0 extra production.
- Reel 3 three days a week (Mon/Wed/Fri) = 7–10 s "one-stat" cut-down from an existing reel.
That is 2–3 reels a day with ~1 production a day. Carousels: 2 a week (Tue, Fri). Stories: daily.
Status codes: READY = built with VO · VO = built, needs voiceover · SCRIPT = script approved, build needed · NEW = to write.

## Daily stories (every day)
Poll or quiz tied to the hero topic (collects real audience data we can quote later), a 1-frame behind-the-scenes of making the reel, and a "DM STORE for a free audit" sticker. Week 1 runs the poll for Episode 21: "Before buying from a new brand's site, do you check their Instagram?"
