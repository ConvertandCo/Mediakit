### 6. 5 shopping "hacks" the evidence doesn't back
- S1: 5 popular e-commerce "hacks". The research doesn't back them.
- S2: "People have an 8-second attention span." The goldfish stat has no traceable source. (BBC, 2017)
- S3: "A countdown timer always boosts sales." A fake timer is false urgency, listed as a dark pattern in India's 2023 CCPA guidelines.
- S4: "A perfect 5.0 wins." Purchase likelihood peaks at 4.0–4.7 stars. (Spiegel, 2017)
- S5: "More choice = more sales." A meta-analysis of 50 choice experiments found no consistent effect either way. (Scheibehenne et al., 2010)
- S6: "Cheaper always sells more." In a catalogue test, a dress sold more at $39 than at $34. (Anderson & Simester)
- S7: What works instead: clear answers, honest pricing, real reviews.
